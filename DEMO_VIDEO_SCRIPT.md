# Demo Video Script — School Management & Online Examination System

Target length: **4–6 minutes**. Record your screen (OBS Studio / Loom / Windows Game Bar / phone screen
recorder all work fine) while following these steps. Narration lines are suggestions — read them naturally,
don't worry about matching word-for-word.

Uses the pre-seeded demo accounts (see `README.md` / `WHATSAPP_SUMMARY.md`), so no setup is needed —
just open the site and go.

---

## 0. Intro (0:00–0:20)

**Show:** Landing page (`/`)

**Say:**
> "Hi, this is a complete School Management and Online Examination System — built for schools that want
> to run everything online: student and teacher records, question banks, live online exams, and results,
> all from one dashboard. Let me show you how it works."

Scroll down briefly to show the 4-panel section and feature list, then click **"Try Demo"**.

---

## 1. Login Screen (0:20–0:35)

**Show:** `/login`

**Say:**
> "Every user — Admin, Teacher, Student, or Parent — logs in here. For this demo, I've added quick-login
> buttons so you can jump straight into any role without typing credentials."

Click the **Admin** demo button (auto-fills email/password) → click **Log in**.

---

## 2. Admin Panel Walkthrough (0:35–2:30)

**Show:** Admin Dashboard

**Say:**
> "This is the Admin dashboard — full control of the school."

Click through the sidebar, briefly on each (2–3 seconds per screen is enough):

1. **Academic Years** — *"You set up academic years here, and mark one as current."*
2. **Classes & Sections** — *"Classes are tied to an academic year, and each class can have multiple sections."*
3. **Subjects** — *"Subjects get assigned to one or more classes."*
4. **Students** — *"Adding a student here automatically creates their login account — the temporary
   password is shown right on screen so you can hand it to them."* (Point at an existing student row.)
5. **Teachers** — *"Same for teachers — and importantly, each teacher is assigned to specific class +
   subject combinations. That controls exactly what they're allowed to manage elsewhere in the system."*
6. **Parents** — *"Parents get linked to their child's student record, so they only ever see their own
   child's results."*
7. **Question Bank** — *"This is where MCQ questions live — tagged by class and subject, with configurable
   marks and negative marking."* Optionally click **Add Question** to show the form live, then cancel.
8. **Exams** — *"Exams are built by pulling questions from the bank. You set the schedule, duration, and
   pass percentage, then publish it."*
9. **Results** — *"Once an exam is finished, you review submissions here — average score, pass rate,
   highest/lowest — and then explicitly Declare the results."*

> "Notice nothing here required a page reload — every list, every form, updates instantly."

---

## 3. Student Takes a Live Exam (2:30–4:00) — the highlight

**Show:** Log out → Log in as **Student** (`student1@school.test`, click demo button)

**Say:**
> "Now let's see it from a student's side. There's a live demo exam ready to take right now."

1. Click **My Exams** → point at **"Live Demo Quiz — Mathematics"** showing status **Live**.
2. Click **Start Exam**.
3. **Say:** *"Notice the countdown timer at the top — it's synced to the real exam deadline. Every
   answer I click is saved instantly, no Save button needed."*
4. Click through 2–3 questions, selecting answers, using **Next** and the question navigator on the right
   (point out the green "answered" indicators).
5. Click **Submit Exam** → confirm.
6. **Say:** *"Once submitted, the exam is locked — no going back to change answers. The score is
   calculated instantly behind the scenes, but it stays hidden from the student until a teacher officially
   declares it — that's an intentional control, not a bug."*

---

## 4. Teacher Declares Results (4:00–4:45)

**Show:** Log out → Log in as **Teacher** (`teacher@school.test`)

1. Click **My Classes** — *"Teachers only ever see their own assigned classes."*
2. Click **Results** → open **"Past Demo Quiz — Science"** (already finished).
3. Point at the **class performance summary** (average / highest / lowest / pass rate).
4. Click **Declare Results** (if not already declared) → confirm.
5. **Say:** *"The moment I declare this, students and parents can immediately see their scores."*

---

## 5. Results & Marksheet (4:45–5:30)

**Show:** Log out → Log in as **Student** or **Parent**

1. Click **My Results** (or **Child's Results** for parent) — show the Pass/Fail badges.
2. Click **View Marksheet** — *"This is a printable report card, aggregating every declared exam across
   subjects, with letter grades and an overall pass/fail."*
3. Click the **Print** button briefly to show the print-friendly layout (cancel the print dialog after).

---

## 6. Closing (5:30–6:00)

**Show:** Back to landing page, or just talk to camera/screen.

**Say:**
> "That's the full loop — from setting up classes and teachers, to a student taking a live exam, to a
> teacher declaring results, to a parent seeing their child's marksheet — all in real time, no page
> reloads anywhere. If you'd like this set up for your school, message me on WhatsApp or email —
> both are on the homepage."

## Recording Tips

- Use **1280×720 or 1920×1080**, keep the browser zoomed to ~100–110% so text is readable.
- Hide bookmarks bar before recording (cleaner look).
- Pause 1–2 seconds after each click before talking, so viewers see the page settle.
- If you flub a line, just pause, take a breath, and redo that sentence — it's easy to cut in editing
  (or just re-record that one screen-recording pass).
- Once you have the final video, upload it to YouTube (unlisted or public) and send me the link —
  I'll embed it directly into the landing page.
