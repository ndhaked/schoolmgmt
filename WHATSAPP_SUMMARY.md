*School Management System – Project Summary*

Built with Laravel 11 + Livewire (real-time, no page reloads).

*4 Panels:*
👨‍💼 *Admin* – Manage academic years, classes/sections, subjects, students, teachers, parents, question bank, exams & results
👩‍🏫 *Teacher* – Manage only their assigned classes/subjects: question bank, exams, results
🎓 *Student* – View exams, take online exams (live timer, auto-save, auto-submit), view results & marksheet
👪 *Parent* – View linked child's results & marksheet

*Key Features:*
✅ Auto login creation for Student/Teacher/Parent
✅ Online MCQ exams with live countdown timer & autosave
✅ Auto-evaluation with negative marking
✅ Results hidden until officially "Declared"
✅ Printable marksheet with grades (A+/A/B/C/F)
✅ Role-based security – no cross-access between users
✅ 86 automated tests for reliability
